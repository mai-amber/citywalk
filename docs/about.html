City Walk

A phone navigation prototype using real Google Street View imagery in Paris.

Open Quest VR: enter VR, turn your head to look, and press either trigger or A/X to follow the street ahead. B/Y exits. Street View uses 360° photographs; physical walking does not move you through the street.

Regular: swipe to look around, pinch to zoom, and tap the street or its arrows to move. Gyro: allow motion access, turn your phone to look, and tap Forward to move.

Privacy

This prototype does not record research sessions or request your location. Phone orientation is processed in your browser to control the view. Google receives requests needed to display Street View, including your IP address and browser information. The hosting service also processes ordinary access requests.

Use is subject to the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

This is an early prototype. Street imagery and available routes can change.
