City Walk

A phone navigation prototype using real Google Street View imagery in Paris.

Open Quest VR: enter VR, turn your head to look, and press either trigger or A/X to follow the street ahead. B/Y exits. Street View uses 360° photographs; physical walking does not move you through the street.

Regular: swipe to look around, pinch to zoom, and tap the street or its arrows to move. Gyro: allow motion access, turn your phone to look, and tap Forward to move.

Privacy

Recording starts automatically when the street opens. Download CSV saves the recordings on this device, including the current session, while navigation continues. Each visit has a unique session ID and an automatically generated code; the code does not identify a person.

Recordings contain timestamps, mode, visited panorama identifiers, viewing direction, navigation actions and technical events. Gyro mode also records device orientation. Quest records available headset and controller poses. There is no eye tracking, microphone recording or GPS request.

Research data stays in this browser's local storage and is not uploaded. Earlier recordings remain available in CSV downloads. Clearing site data, using a private window or uninstalling the browser can remove these local copies. Export the CSV to keep it. The Quest APK keeps its recordings on the headset and exports to Downloads / CityWalk.

Google receives requests needed to display Street View, including your IP address and browser information. The hosting service also processes ordinary access requests.

Use is subject to the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

This is an early prototype. Street imagery and available routes can change.
